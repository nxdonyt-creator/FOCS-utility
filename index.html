NVIDIA profiles, corrected.

Version 3.4.0 corrects the Potato page to edit Fortnite’s NVIDIA driver profile. The deeper engineering review explains the next safety and measurement improvements.

01 / INSPECT

Inspect the NVIDIA profile

Read Fortnite’s saved driver settings, then choose texture LOD +1.5 or +3.0 with high-performance filtering and a 1x anisotropic override.

02 / COMPARE

Separate driver and game settings

The NVIDIA Potato action has its own Apply and Reset. The original INI editor is clearly labelled In-game low graphics in an optional section.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Know what remains unverified

Current Fortnite DX12 may ignore driver texture overrides. Saved values do not prove changed visuals or better FPS. Compare the actual result before keeping a profile.
